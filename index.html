About

From a shared room in Geelong to a 9-to-5 I'm still figuring out.

I finished my civil engineering degree in India and moved to Australia in 2020 — landing in a shared room in Geelong with no real plan beyond "make it work."

Since then I've completed my Master's, worked as a project manager on infrastructure projects, and now sit inside a PMO office supporting programs with operating assets worth over $3.6 billion. It's been a genuinely wild ride.

Outside the 9-to-5, you'll find me behind a camera, on a motorbike, or in the kitchen — and increasingly, thinking about how to build financial freedom instead of just clocking in.
